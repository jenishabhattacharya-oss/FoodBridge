Who We Are

A bridge between surplus and supper.

We are a community-run network of donors, volunteers and verified NGOs working on one simple idea: good food should feed people, not landfills.

Mission

Rescue every edible surplus meal and route it to a table that needs it, safely and within the hour.

  • RescueSurplus listed by hosts and kitchens in under a minute.
  • ScreenPhoto checks by AI, with human review when unsure.
  • RedistributeVolunteers deliver to verified NGOs with photo proof.

Focus Areas

Four programs. One food loop.

Each program closes a gap between a kitchen with too much and a community with too little.

01

Surplus Rescue & Rapid Dispatch

Donors list leftovers with portions, pickup window and a map pin. The nearest available volunteer is matched to the run.

  • Listing in about 60 seconds
  • One active run per volunteer
02

Gemini AI Food Safety Screening

Overview, close-up and label photos are checked for visible warning signs before a listing goes live.

  • Uncertain items go to human review
  • Allergen and storage notes captured
03

Verified NGO & Shelter Redistribution

Only approved organisations can accept food. They confirm receipt with photo proof, closing the chain of custody.

  • Staff-reviewed NGO onboarding
  • Receipt photos on every delivery
04

Carbon Mitigation & ESG Impact

Every completed delivery adds to a transparent record of meals redirected and emissions avoided for donors.

  • Meals and weight tracked
  • Audit trail for CSR reporting
How It Works

From surplus to shared table.

A frictionless handoff designed to move food from banquet halls to community centres in minutes, not hours.

01

List surplus in 60 seconds

Hosts and chefs add portions, food type, pickup window and three quick photos with a location pin.

02

AI screening & NGO match

Gemini checks the photos for visible warning signs while nearby verified NGOs are offered the listing.

03

Volunteer handoff with proof

A nearby volunteer claims the run, navigates to the donor, and closes the loop with a delivery photo.

You upload an overview, a close-up and, for packaged food, a label photo. The AI flags visible warning signs; anything it cannot assess confidently goes to a human reviewer. It is visual screening only, not a food-safety certification, so donors remain responsible for what they list.

Registered volunteers in your city claim available pickups. Each volunteer can hold one active run at a time, navigates to you and the receiving NGO, and uploads a delivery photo to complete the handoff.
